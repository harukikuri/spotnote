## Write a note

1. In selection mode, click an element. The note panel opens.
2. Type your request. `Shift+Enter` adds a line break.
3. Press `Enter`, or click <SendButton label="Add & copy" />.

The note is saved, its pin appears, and the prompt is on your clipboard.

<Callout type="warn" title="Closing first throws the draft away">
  Closing the panel with <PanelButton icon="close" /> or `Esc` before step 3 discards what you typed.
</Callout>

### Clicking away with unsent text

If you click elsewhere on the page while the panel holds unsent text, the panel shakes and keeps your text. Send it with `Enter`, or close it with <PanelButton icon="close" /> or `Esc`.

## Edit a note

Click its pin, or click its element in selection mode. The panel opens with the note filled in.

- **Changes save when the panel closes**, however you close it.
- `Enter` (or <SendButton label="Save & copy" />) saves and copies the updated prompt.
- <PanelButton icon="copy" /> at the top copies without closing the panel.

Clicking away with unsaved changes makes the panel shake, as for a new note.

## Delete notes

**One note:** open it, click <PanelButton icon="trash" />, then **Delete** (or **Cancel**). The remaining pins are renumbered.

**Several notes:** open the notes list, then:

- tick the ones to delete and click <PanelButton icon="trash" count="(2)" /> at the top (it shows how many), or
- with none ticked, click <PanelButton icon="trash" count="(all)" /> to delete every note.

Spotnote asks first (**Delete all 3 notes?**). Click **Delete** or **Cancel**.

## The notes list

Open the launcher and click <LauncherItem icon="notes" />. Click a note to scroll to its element and open it. If it's on another page of your app, Spotnote goes there first.

- This page's notes come first, under **This page**. Other pages follow, under their paths.
- Numbers match the pins.
- Click a heading to fold that section (it shows how many notes it holds). Click again to unfold.

## Pins

- Pins show while the launcher is open, and hide when it's closed.
- Click a pin to open its note. Click it again to close it.

## When your code changes

A note finds its element by the element's **source location** (file, line, column), plus its position for repeated elements.

| Your edit | The pin |
| --- | --- |
| Doesn't move the element's opening tag (classes, text, children) | Stays. |
| Moves the tag to another line or column (say, a new line above it) | Disappears. The note is marked **Not found** in the notes list. |

From the notes list, a **Not found** note can still be:

- **copied**: you get its last prompt, plus a line telling the agent the location may have moved;
- **deleted**: for example, once the agent has made the change.

It can't be opened. If the element comes back to the same location (say, you undo the edit), the note finds it again.

## Where notes are stored

In your browser's `localStorage`. They survive reloads and dev-server restarts. Nothing is sent to a server.

They belong to one browser and one address: another browser, or the same app on another port, starts with no notes. See [Storage](/reference/storage).
