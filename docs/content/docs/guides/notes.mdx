## Write a note

1. In selection mode, click an element. The note panel opens.
2. Type your request. `Shift+Enter` adds a line break.
3. Press `Enter`, or click <SendButton label="Add & copy" />.

<img src="/notes-write.png" alt="A button selected, with the note panel open below it: span .btn Home.jsx:17, and the typed request Make this button bigger next to Add & copy" width="348" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

The note is saved, its pin appears, and the prompt is on your clipboard.

## One note on several elements

For a request about more than one element, such as "make these two buttons match", [pick several elements](/guides/picking-elements#pick-several-elements), then write the note and press `Enter`.

Each element gets a pin with the same number, and any of them opens the note. The prompt has a `## Target` section for each element, in the order you picked them.

Once your agent has the note, its elements stay as they were sent.

## Edit a note

Click its pin, or click its element in selection mode. The panel opens with the note filled in.

<img src="/notes-edit.png" alt="Pin 2 on a button, with its note panel open and filled in: Make this pill-shaped" width="380" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

- **Changes save when the panel closes**, however you close it.
- `Enter` (or <SendButton label="Save & copy" />) saves and copies the updated prompt.
- <PanelButton icon="copy" /> at the top copies without closing the panel.

Clicking away with unsaved changes makes the panel shake.

## Delete notes

**One note:** open it, click <PanelButton icon="trash" />, then **Delete** (or **Cancel**). The remaining pins are renumbered.

**Several notes:** open the notes list, then:

- tick the ones to delete and click <PanelButton icon="trash" count="(2)" /> at the top (it shows how many), or
- with none ticked, click <PanelButton icon="trash" /> to delete every note.

Spotnote asks first (**Delete all 3 notes?**). Click **Delete** or **Cancel**.

<img src="/notes-delete.png" alt="The notes list with two notes ticked, asking: Delete 2 notes? This can't be undone. Cancel or Delete" width="496" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

## The notes list

Open the launcher and click <LauncherItem icon="notes" />. Click a note to scroll to its element and open it. If it's on another page of your app, Spotnote goes there first.

<img src="/notes-list.png" alt="The notes list: two notes under This page, one under /products, each with its pin number and a chip with its element, file and line" width="496" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

- This page's notes come first, under **This page**. Other pages follow, under their paths.
- Numbers match the pins.
- Click a heading to fold that section (it shows how many notes it holds). Click again to unfold.

## Note status

With [your agent connected](/guides/mcp), each pin shows where its note stands:

| Pin | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <PinIcon n={1} /> | Waiting for an agent. |
| <PinIcon state="working" /> | The agent is working on it. |
| <PinIcon state="asked" /> | The agent asked you something. Answer in the panel. |
| <PinIcon state="done" /> | Done. The note shows what changed. |
| <PinIcon state="dismissed" /> | The agent won't make the change. The note shows why. |
