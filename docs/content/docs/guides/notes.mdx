---
title: Notes
description: Write, edit, copy and delete notes, and what happens to them when your code changes.
---

A **note** is the text you write about an element. A **pin** is the numbered marker that Spotnote leaves on that element so you can find the note again. One element has at most one note.

## Writing a note

1. In [selection mode](/guides/picking-elements), click an element. The note panel opens.
2. Type your request. `Shift+Enter` adds a line break.
3. Press `Enter`, or click **↵ Add & copy**.

The note is saved, its pin appears, and the prompt is on your clipboard.

Closing the panel before that (with **×** or `Esc`) throws the draft away.

### Clicking away with unsent text

If you click somewhere else on the page while the panel holds text you haven't sent, the panel shakes and keeps your text. Send it with `Enter`, or close the panel with **×** or `Esc`.

## Editing a note

Click its pin, or click the element itself in selection mode. The panel opens with the note filled in.

- **Your changes save when the panel closes**, however you close it.
- `Enter` (or **↵ Save & copy**) saves and copies the updated prompt in one step.
- The **copy** button at the top copies the prompt without closing the panel.

While your [coding agent is connected](/guides/mcp), notes go to it directly: the panel says **↵ Add** / **↵ Save**, the copy button is hidden, and `Enter` leaves your clipboard alone.

Clicking away with unsaved changes makes the panel shake, the same as for a new note.

## Deleting a note

Open the note and click the **trash** button. The pin goes away and the remaining pins are renumbered.

## The notes list

Open the launcher and press **Notes** to see every note, numbered like its pin. Click one to scroll to its element and open it. If the note was written on another page of your app, Spotnote navigates there first.

The list puts this page's notes first, under **This page**, and each other page's under its path. The numbers stay the same as on the pins.

## Pins

- Pins show while the launcher is open, and hide when it's closed.
- Click a pin to open its note. Click it again to close it.

## When your code changes

A note is tied to its element by the element's source location (file, line and column) plus, for repeated elements, its position among them. Hot reload keeps that link as long as the location stays the same.

- **Edits that don't move the element's opening tag** (changing its classes, text or children) keep the pin in place.
- **Edits that move the tag to another line or column** (for example, adding a line above it) break the link. The pin disappears, and the note is marked **Not found** in the notes list.

A note marked **Not found** can't be opened, but from the notes list you can:

- **copy** it: you get the prompt saved the last time the note was sent or edited, with a line telling the agent the location may have moved;
- **delete** it, for example once the agent has made the change.

If the element comes back to the same location (say, you undo the edit), the note finds it again.

## Where notes are stored

Notes are saved in your browser's `localStorage`. They survive reloads and dev-server restarts, but they belong to that browser and that address: a different browser, or the same app on another port, starts with no notes. Nothing is sent to a server. See [Storage](/reference/storage).
