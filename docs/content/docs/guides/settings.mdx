## MCP

Shows whether your agent is connected through [spotnote-mcp](/guides/mcp), updated live.

<img src="/settings-agent.png" alt="The MCP page in Settings: Not connected, with the two steps to connect Claude Code" width="448" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Connected | Your agent picks up notes as you write them. |
| Connecting… | It just started. Your notes are syncing. |
| Not connected | No agent is running spotnote-mcp. |

Click the row for its page. When you're not connected, it shows the two steps to connect Claude Code, each with a copy button.

## Accent color

The color of the outline, labels and pins. Yellow is the default. Switch to orange, pink, violet, blue or green when yellow blends into your app.

<img src="/settings-accent.png" alt="Settings with blue picked as the accent color, and the launcher turned blue" width="453" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

## Theme

The icon at the top right of Settings. Click it to cycle: **System** (follows your OS), **Light** (the default), **Dark**.

<img src="/settings-theme.png" alt="Settings and the launcher in the Dark theme, with the moon icon at the top right" width="453" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

## Open files in

Where a file opens when you click its name, in the note panel's header or in the agent's messages (`router.jsx:28`).

<img src="/settings-editor.png" alt="Settings with Open files in set to Cursor" width="453" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Auto** (default) | Opens in the editor that's running. If that editor's command-line tool isn't installed (VS Code's `code`, for example), opens through the editor's URL instead. |
| **VS Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Zed** | Always opens in that editor through its URL (`vscode://file/…`). Works without the editor running or its command-line tool installed. The browser may ask permission the first time. |

**If Auto finds no editor**, a warning icon appears next to the link. Hover it for why, then pick your editor here. To name an editor Auto doesn't recognise, set `LAUNCH_EDITOR` when starting your dev server.

<img src="/settings-no-editor.png" alt="A warning icon next to Home.jsx:15 in the note panel, with its tooltip: No editor is running. Pick yours in Settings → Open files in." width="356" className="my-6 rounded-lg border border-fd-border shadow-sm" />
