## Start picking

Pick whichever fits what you're doing:

| Way | How | Stays on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Launcher** | Click <LauncherIcon /> in a corner. | Until you pause it or close the launcher. |
| **`Alt` key** | Hold `Alt` (`⌥ Option` on a Mac). | Only while you hold the key. Good for a quick pick while you use your app. |

Either way, **selection mode** starts: an outline follows your cursor, and clicking an element opens its note panel instead of reaching your app.

## The launcher

- **Click it** to open it: selection mode starts, it turns yellow, and your pins appear.
- <LauncherIcon open /> closes it: selection mode ends and the pins hide.
- **Drag it** to move it. It snaps to the nearest corner and remembers it.

It always starts closed when the page loads. Opening it shows three buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pause** | Turns selection mode off and back on. Your pins stay visible, so you can use your app with them. The launcher turns grey while paused. |
| **Notes** | Opens [the list of your notes](/guides/notes#the-notes-list). |
| **Settings** | Opens [Settings](/guides/settings). |

## Back out with `Esc`

Each press closes one thing, front first:

1. the note panel, the notes list or Settings (whichever you used last)
2. the other one, if it's open
3. selection mode (the launcher stays open, paused)

When panels overlap, click the one behind to bring it to the front.

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="What happens to clicks while the note panel is open">

Clicks still don't reach your app. Clicking another element moves the panel to it. Clicking empty space closes the panel.

If the panel holds text you haven't sent, it shakes and keeps your text instead. See [Notes](/guides/notes#clicking-away-with-unsent-text).

</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Which element a click selects">

Spotnote picks the element that carries a **stamp**: the `data-spotnote` attribute the plugin adds to every element written in your source.

If you click something without one (for example, markup rendered inside a dependency), the selection moves up to the nearest ancestor that has one, and the prompt says so.

</Accordion>
</Accordions>
