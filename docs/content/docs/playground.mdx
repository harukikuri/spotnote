Try Spotnote on a small shop, before you install it: **[open the Playground](/try)**, or click **Try it** at the top of any page.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the launcher

Click <LauncherIcon /> at the bottom left of the shop.

</Step>
<Step>

### Click an element

Hover over the shop: an outline follows the cursor, with the element's file and line. Click the one you want to change.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the change, press `Enter`

The prompt is on your clipboard. Paste it anywhere to see it, or into your agent.

</Step>
</Steps>

The shop is built with Spotnote in it, the way a [staging build](/guides/staging) is, so its prompts name the commit too. Its source is in the repository: [`playground/`](https://github.com/harukikuri/spotnote/tree/main/playground). Notes stay in your browser.

Agents can't take notes from here: spotnote-mcp runs on your machine, next to your own dev server. See [Connect Your Agent](/guides/mcp).
