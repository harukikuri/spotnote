## Creating a note

1. Click the **Select element** button in the Spotnote panel
2. Click an element on the page (hold **Shift** to multi-select)
3. Type your feedback in the note panel — it saves automatically to localStorage

## Editing a note

Click on an existing note marker to open it. Edit the text directly — changes save automatically.

## Deleting a note

Open a note and click the delete button to remove it. This action cannot be undone.

## Storage

All notes are stored in your browser's localStorage under the key `spotnote-notes`. They persist across page reloads and browser restarts, but are local to your browser — they won't sync across devices or team members.

When you copy a note, the clipboard content is formatted as markdown:

````markdown
## Feedback [git:a1b2c3d] [route:/]

Make this title bigger
````
